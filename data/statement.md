## Accessibility

This statement applies to **keyzh.pages.dev** and its localized routes.

### Commitment

This website is designed and maintained to support inclusive access for people with different abilities and assistive technology needs.

### Conformance Status

This site aims to align with [WCAG 2.2 Level AA](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/). Some content or interactions may still be under improvement, and ongoing updates are made as issues are identified.

### Measures Taken

- Semantic HTML structure for major sections.
- Keyboard-focusable controls and visible focus states.
- Readable type scale and contrast-aware text tones.
- Ongoing automated accessibility checks in development.

### Feedback and Support

If you encounter an accessibility barrier, please email [keyouzheng0915@gmail.com](mailto:keyouzheng0915@gmail.com). Include the page URL, the issue details, and your assistive technology setup when possible.

## Privacy

This privacy statement applies to **keyzh.pages.dev** and its localized routes.

### What We Track

This website uses **Umami** analytics to understand aggregate traffic trends such as:

- Total visitors
- Page views
- Referrers
- Device and browser categories

### What We Do Not Collect

We do not use analytics for advertising profiles or cross-site behavioral tracking.

No directly identifying personal data is intentionally collected through Umami event tracking on this site.

### Why We Track

Analytics data is used only to improve site content, structure, and accessibility quality.

### Local Preferences

Your theme and sound choices may be saved in your browser so the website can remember them. These preferences stay on your device and are not used for analytics or advertising.

### Visitor Number in Footer

The footer visitor number is fetched from Umami aggregated website statistics and shown as a simple count.

### Data Access and Retention

Analytics data is stored and processed by Umami and its configured infrastructure.

For platform-level data handling details, please refer to Umami's own privacy and terms documentation.
